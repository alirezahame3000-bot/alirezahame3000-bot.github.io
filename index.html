Collect, organize, and export webpage images.

A browser extension designed for image-heavy pages and comic workflows. It detects images in page order, lets users download them together, and can turn selected images into a compressed PDF document.

Ordered image detection

Find images on the current page and preserve their reading order.

Bulk download

Save selected images together instead of downloading them one by one.

Comic workflow

Arrange page images for personal comic and image-document projects.

Compressed PDF export

Combine selected images into a compact PDF for easier storage and reading.
