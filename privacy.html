Privacy Policy

This page describes the privacy practices for the browser extension presented on this website.

Page content

The extension may read images and related webpage resources on pages where the user chooses to use its image collection, download, or PDF-export features.

Local processing

Image organization, download preparation, and document creation are intended to be performed for the user's requested workflow. The extension is not designed to sell personal information or use webpage content for advertising.

Third-party services

If paid features are introduced, a third-party payment or licensing provider may process purchase and license information under that provider's own privacy terms. This policy will be updated if those integrations materially change the data handled by the extension.

User responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that their downloading and use of webpage content complies with applicable law, copyright, and the terms of the source website.

Updates

This policy may be updated as the extension's features or integrations change.
